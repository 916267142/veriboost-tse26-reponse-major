\begin{generalcomment}
All reviewers agree that the manuscript requires a major revision, 
but that it is worth to have a next review stage. 

1. The paper needs a major technical revision that accurately defines
the verification guarantees, reviews the issues raised about the
theorems by the reviewers, as well as all other technical aspects in
the reviews.

2. The paper requires a better positioning of the journal extensions
wrt the FM paper, not only in the letter, but rather in the article
text. Related work has to be expanded as well.

3. Experimental evaluation should use real-world complex routing
configurations, otherwise there is no evidence supporting the
practical applicability of the proposed method. Overall, the
interpretation of the evaluation results needs to be strengthened.

4. The paper has to clarify its key innovations and theoretical
contributions compared with existing network verification acceleration
methods. In particular, the applicability of the considered
assumptions in complex real-world WANs is not clear.
\end{generalcomment}
